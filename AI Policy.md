# WHATWG AI Policy

## Using AI responsibly

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to any software that can be used to evaluate, transform, or generate content that would otherwise be reviewed or analyzed by a human. Examples include machine learning (ML), large language models (LLMs), or generative AI (GenAI).

The purpose of this policy is to provide guidance on the use of AI when contributing to WHATWG Living Standards, e.g., proposed specification edits, bug reports, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, or other contributions, as well as during WHATWG meetings. These guidelines may be revised as available AI tools continue to evolve.

## Contributing to Living Standards

### Review all contributions

You are responsible for the correctness of all code, documentation, issues, and comments you submit, regardless of whether you used AI tools. You are expected to be able to answer any questions reviewers may have about your proposed changes.

Contributions that are generated by AI require meaningful human review before submission. Your submission is expected to be at or above the level of quality you would have produced working diligently on your own. Please review contributions and modify as needed before requesting peer review. You should be able to attest that the work is your own, even if AI produced the first draft.

### Make contributions easy to review

The time of reviewers is limited. Therefore:

* Keep generated text as short as possible.
* Include evidence where applicable, e.g., a link to source code or a test case.
* Keep each issue or pull request focused on a single problem or change.
* Learn from review feedback. If you use AI tools, consider updating their instructions or configuration based on the feedback you receive so that the same mistakes are not repeated.

### Disclose AI use

You are encouraged, but not required, to disclose use of AI tools. To aid reviewers, please flag any areas where you are not fully confident in the accuracy of AI-generated content, either in a comment or in the pull request description. You may also disclose the AI tool used and prompt provided. If you supplied additional materials to the AI tool or worked through multiple iterations, a brief summary of your process would be appreciated.

### Respect intellectual property rights

Do not input copyrighted or third-party-owned material into AI tools without authorization, such as permission from the owner or a license that permits it. For example, the WHATWG Living Standards have a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License and may be used as input.

Your proposed changes to Living Standards remain subject to the [IPR Policy](./IPR%20Policy.md), whether or not you used AI tools.

### Protect confidential information

Do not input personally identifiable information, confidential materials, or other sensitive data into AI tools.

## Meetings

WHATWG meeting scribes may use AI tools to generate transient summaries to reference when creating official meeting minutes. Inform all meeting participants before using AI for this purpose and delete any AI-generated summaries after the official meeting minutes have been approved.

## Contact 

Please direct questions relating to this policy to sg@whatwg.org.
